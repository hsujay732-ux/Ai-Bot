Agar tum Binance futures trade karte ho,
toh fake walls pe paisa gawana band karo.
Entry se pehle dekho ki wall pe actually kya ho raha hai.

Is video mein main dikhaunga ki DOM wall bounce kaise kaam karta hai,
aur entry se pehle woh ek check jo zyada tar log skip kar dete hain.

Wall matlab order book ka woh level jahan size baaki levels se kaafi zyada hai.
Main wall tab maanta hoon jab size average ka 10 guna ho.
Bid side pe wall: possible support.
Ask side pe wall: possible resistance.

Sabse common galti: price ne wall touch kiya, aur turant buy.
Problem ye hai ki wall ek order hai, promise nahi.
Price paas aate hi wall pull ho sakti hai,
aur tum bina support ke trade mein phase ho.

Fake wall ka sign dekho.
Price jaise jaise paas aata hai, wall ka size ghatne lagta hai,
ya wall ek level aage-peeche shift hoti rehti hai.
Real wall wahi khadi rehti hai jab price usse takrata hai.

Ab woh check jiska maine shuru mein promise kiya tha.
Touch pe tape dekho.
Market sells wall pe hit ho rahe hain,
lekin wall ka size kam nahi ho raha, ya wapas refill ho raha hai.
Iska matlab koi seller ko absorb kar raha hai.
Yahi bounce ka asli signal hai.

Absorption ke baad price ko wall se upar uthne do.
Delta buyers ki taraf flip ho, tab entry lo.
Touch pe nahi, confirmation pe.

Stop wall ke thoda neeche rakho, 2 ticks.
Agar wall tooti, toh setup khatam.
Wahan rukne ka koi reason nahi.
